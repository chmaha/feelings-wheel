Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The short version

Feelings Wheel does not collect, send or sell any personal information. Everything you add stays on your own device.

What the app stores

To work, the app saves the following in your device's local storage:

  • the feelings you add to your list, with the date and time you added them;
  • any optional notes you write about those feelings;
  • your settings, such as view, theme and language.

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to copy or share it yourself.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
  • No data is sent to us or to any third party.

External links

The app links to findahelpline.com for crisis support. If you open that link, that website's own privacy policy applies.

Deleting your data

Use “Clear list” in My list to remove your feelings and notes. Uninstalling the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes everything the app has stored.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue on GitHub.
